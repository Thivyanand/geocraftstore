Refund policy.

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Digital purchases

Because ranks are digital goods, refund requests are reviewed individually. If you were charged but payment verification failed, or a technical issue prevented delivery, contact us with your order or payment reference so we can investigate and arrange delivery or an appropriate refund.

Incorrect username

If a rank was purchased for the wrong Minecraft username, contact support promptly. We will review whether the rank can be moved before it has been used. Transfers are not guaranteed.

How to contact us

Email support@geocraft.live with your Minecraft username and transaction reference. We aim to acknowledge requests within 3 business days.
