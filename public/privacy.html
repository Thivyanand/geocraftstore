STORE POLICIES

Privacy policy.

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Information we collect

When an order is placed, we store the Minecraft Java username, selected digital product, amount, currency, order and payment identifiers, and order status. We keep operational audit records to investigate payment and delivery issues.

Payments

Razorpay processes payment details. GeoCraft does not receive or store raw card details. Payment credentials and server access credentials are held only in the store's private server environment.

Use and retention

Order information is used to verify purchases, deliver ranks, provide support, and meet applicable record keeping requirements. Contact us to request access or correction: support@geocraft.live.
